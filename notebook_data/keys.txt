The following is the full text of the diagnostic identification keys for the genus Nacaduba:

1 (22): Underside forewing without a pair of pale subbasal lines in the cell. (pavana group).
2 (3): Underside hindwing with a prominent rounded black submarginal spot in space 6. Fwl 14.0–15.5 mm. ... Nacaduba angusta
3: Underside hindwing without a prominent submarginal spot in space 6.
4 (7): Underside postdiscal band composed of a double series of pale striae inwardly bounded by darker lines with the space between them paler than the ground colour.
5 (6): ♂ upperside pale greyish blue, border a thread. ♀ upperside brown, with a wedge-shaped, pale blue discal patch on the forewing and basal blue dusting on the hindwing. Fwl 11.0–12.0 mm. ... Nacaduba pavana
6: ♂ rather pale purple blue, border inwardly diffuse. ♀ as in N. pavana but blue patch darker. Fwl 11.0–12.0 mm. ... Nacaduba russelli
7: Underside with the area between the postdiscal striae unicolorous and of the ground colour.
8 (9): Underside striae broad, diffuse and yellowish. Fwl 14.5–16.0 mm. ... Nacaduba pactolus
9: Underside striae narrow and well defined, slightly wider in ♀ than in ♂.
10 (13): Underside hindwing with inner submarginal and outer postdiscal striae in spaces 4 and 5 nearly touching; striae pale buff in ♂, whitish in ♀.
11 (12): ♂ upperside violet blue, with a slightly frosted appearance, border a thread. ♀ upperside pale shining blue, with only a very slight purple tinge. Underside hindwing tornal black spot in space 2 crowned by a brownish-orange ring. Fwl 13.0–15.0 mm. ... Nacaduba hermus
12: ♂ upperside bluish purple, border a thread. ♀ blue areas slightly deeper and more purple than in N. hermus. Underside hindwing tornal black spot in space 2 crowned by a clear orange ring. Fwl 13.0–15.0 mm. ... Nacaduba subperusia lysa
13: Underside hindwing with inner submarginal and outer postdiscal striae separated by at least half the width of the postdiscal band.
14 (15): ♂ upperside purplish blue, with the border expanding to at least 1.0 mm at the forewing apex. ♀ upperside paler, less shining blue, with rather narrow borders, Fwl 14.0–16.0 mm. ... Nacaduba pendleburyi
15: ♂ upperside forewing border a thread throughout.
16 (17): Underside forewing postdiscal band continuous and almost straight from vein 3 to vein 7. ♂ upperside purple, with a slight bronzy tint. ♀ upperside similar to N. pendleburyi, but blue area darker and more shining. Fwl 15.0–16.0 mm. ... Nacaduba solta
17: Underside forewing postdiscal band curved in towards base and often slightly dislocated at vein 6.
18 (19): ♂ upperside dull purple blue, with the veins darker and contrasting; underside striae pale buff. ♀ upperside more purple than in lysa. Fwl 15.0–16.0 mm. ... Nacaduba subperusia intricata
19: ♂ upperside veins not contrasting; underside striae whitish or white.
20 (21): ♂ upperside purple, with a steely grey tint. ♀ unknown. Fwl 15.0–16.0 mm. Borneo. ... Nacaduba asaga
21: ♂ upperside shining violet. ♀ upperside pale shining silvery blue, with a slight greenish tint and broad black borders. Fwl 13.0–14.5 mm. ... Nacaduba sanaya
22: Underside forewing with a pair of pale subbasal lines across the cell. (berenice group).
23 (24): Underside with the area between the postdiscal striae unicolorous and of the ground colour. ♂ upperside purple, with the underside markings not visible by transparency. ♀ upperside forewing with a purple-blue patch and with bluish spots on the edge of the dark border in spaces 1b and 2. Fwl 12.0–15.0 mm. ... Nacaduba beroe
24: Underside with the postdiscal striae inwardly bounded by darker lines and the space between them paler than the ground colour (but character weakly developed in N. kurava ♂).
25 (26): ♂ upperside violet blue, with underside markings visible by transparency. ♀ upperside pale shining blue turning whitish beyond the forewing cell, border rather narrow. Fwl 13.5–15.0 mm. ... Nacaduba kurava
26: ♂ upperside with underside markings barely visible by transparency. ♀ upperside not whitish beyond the forewing cell, hindwing mostly brown.
27 (30): ♂ upperside pale frosted blue due to the presence of many white ribbon scales. Fwl 11.0–13.0 mm.
28 (29): ♂ upperside pale lavender blue. ♀ upperside forewing with a pale purple blue patch. ... Nacaduba berenice
29: ♂ upperside slightly more violet, but difficult to separate from N. berenice except by genitalia. ♀ unknown. ... Nacaduba kirtoni
30: ♂ upperside bluish violet, without ribbon scales. ♀ upperside forewing discal patch darker blue. ... Nacaduba calauria
